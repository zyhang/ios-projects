## Get help

**Stillwall** blocks ads, trackers, and annoyances in **Safari** on iPhone and iPad.

### Enable protection

1. Open **Stillwall**.  
2. Follow **Set Up Safari Protection**.  
3. In **Settings → Safari → Extensions**, enable **both** Stillwall extensions and allow website access as shown in the app.  
4. Return to Stillwall — Home should show protection **On**.

### Site is broken

- Turn **off** the global switch in Stillwall, **or**  
- Open the **Stillwall Safari extension** on that site and choose **Pause on this site**, then **Resume** when finished.

### YouTube & X ads

Pro blocking for YouTube and X applies in **Safari websites only**, not the native YouTube or X apps.

### Tap to Block

Available with **Stillwall Pro**. Use the Safari extension → **Tap to Block**, then select elements on the page.

### Subscriptions

Manage trials and **Stillwall Pro** in  
**Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**.
