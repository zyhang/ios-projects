‹ Help Site broken?

Fix a broken site

Open the Stillwall extension in Safari and choose Pause on this site.

Or turn off the related category switches (or Strict Mode) in the app. When you’re ready, choose Resume on this site in the extension.

Home does not show an On/Off protection badge—your category switches are the controls.
