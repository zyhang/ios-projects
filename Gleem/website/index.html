The premise

Ads, pop-ups, trackers, cookie warnings—the things that make the web slow and ugly—are removed before the page finishes loading.

Websites in Safari look clean, load lighter, and stop invisibly following you around. A short guided setup—turn on two Safari extensions once—and Stillwall works in the background from then on. When a page needs a pass, pause it from the extension, or turn off the relevant category in the app. That is the whole interface.

Corrections & clarifications

What Stillwall is not

We would rather be precise than overpromise.

§ 1

Safari only

Stillwall works in Safari on iPhone and iPad. It is not system-wide ad blocking—other apps keep their own ads.

§ 2

Not a VPN

Your traffic is never routed anywhere, and your IP is never hidden. Just clean, declarative content blocking.

§ 3

Nothing to collect

No account, no browsing history uploaded, no analytics or ad SDKs inside the app. Your rules live on your device.
