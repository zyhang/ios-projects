The idea

The web is loud. Your browser doesn’t have to be.

You open a page to read something. Not to negotiate with a cookie banner, dodge a video ad, or wonder which script is following you to the next tab.

Stillwall is a Safari content blocker for iPhone and iPad. Turn it on in a few steps— then let it work. Free core protection covers ads, trackers, annoyances, and regional rules. Pro tools wait until you ask for them.

Boundaries

Clear about the edges.

Stillwall works in Safari. It does not block ads inside other apps, and it is not a VPN. YouTube & X Pro blocking is for Safari websites—not the native apps. That honesty is part of the product.
